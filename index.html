Keepers & 2026 Eligibility

The rules

  • A player may be kept for a total of 3 years with the same original contract: the draft year counts as year 1, and a team can keep him again for year 2, then a final time for year 3.
  • After year 3, that contract is used up — the player returns to the draft pool (any team can draft him fresh, restarting at year 1).
  • If a player is traded, his contract year count carries over to the new team unchanged.
  • If a player is dropped and becomes a free agent, his contract resets — whoever adds him later starts back at year 1.
  • If a player is dropped and re-claimed off waivers (not released to true free agency), he stays under his original contract and year count.
